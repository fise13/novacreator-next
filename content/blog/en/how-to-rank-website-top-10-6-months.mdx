## Why “top rankings in 6 months” is possible only with the right baseline

SEO works when the website is ready to convert incoming traffic. If your landing pages do not communicate clear value, trust, and next actions, higher rankings alone will not generate pipeline.

For service businesses in Kazakhstan, the winning sequence is usually: commercial pages first, local intent coverage second, and strategic content clusters third.

## Month 1: Technical foundation

The first month is about removing blockers:

- canonical and hreflang consistency;
- clean sitemap and robots directives;
- duplicate pages and soft-404 cleanup;
- baseline schema coverage;
- Core Web Vitals fixes on key landing pages.

This creates a crawlable, indexable structure Google can trust.

## Month 2: Commercial semantics and page mapping

Most projects fail by targeting random keywords. We map demand by intent:

- transactional queries for service pages;
- local queries for city/region pages;
- problem/solution queries for educational content;
- comparison queries for decision-stage users.

Then we align URL architecture with hub-and-spoke logic to avoid cannibalization.

## Month 3-4: Content that supports revenue

High-performing SEO content for a commercial website should do three things:

1. solve a specific user question;
2. connect that question to business impact;
3. move the reader to a relevant service page.

For example, a technical SEO checklist article should naturally route to [/seo](/seo) and implementation-focused service pages.

## Month 5: Behavior and conversion optimization

At this stage we improve:

- trust components (case studies, numbers, reviews);
- objection-handling FAQ blocks;
- contextual CTAs;
- content template speed and consistency.

This is where organic traffic quality usually improves, not just volume.

## Month 6: Scale what is already working

Instead of starting new experiments from scratch, scale proven clusters:

- expand successful topic clusters;
- refresh pages using Search Console patterns;
- rewrite snippets to increase CTR;
- strengthen internal links between blog, services, and portfolio pages.

Now SEO becomes an operating system, not a one-time campaign.

## KPIs that matter for commercial SEO

Track business outcomes, not only positions:

- indexed URL coverage;
- CTR on commercial queries;
- organic leads and cost per qualified lead;
- branded vs non-branded demand share;
- conversion rate of organic landing pages.
