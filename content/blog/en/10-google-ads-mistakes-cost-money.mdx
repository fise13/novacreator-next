## Mistake 1: Mixing different search intents in one campaign

When “buy now”, “what is”, and “reviews” keywords are grouped together, relevance drops and cost rises. Build campaigns by intent first.

## Mistake 2: Conversion tracking is not validated

Many accounts optimize toward broken events. Before launch, confirm:

- lead form submit;
- phone/WhatsApp clicks;
- micro-conversions;
- GA4 + Ads event consistency.

## Mistake 3: Negative keyword list is static

Search terms evolve quickly. Weekly search-term reviews usually unlock immediate budget savings.

## Mistake 4: Generic ads across all segments

Message-to-intent mismatch lowers CTR and quality score. Ad copy should mirror specific intent and route users to dedicated landing pages.

## Mistake 5: Landing page mismatch

If the ad promise is “SEO agency in Almaty” but the landing page is generic, bounce rate grows and conversion drops.

## Mistake 6: Smart bidding too early

Automated bidding can work well, but only after stable conversion signals. Early-stage accounts often perform better with controlled bidding strategies.

## Mistake 7: No remarketing flow

Most visitors do not convert on the first session. Without remarketing, high-intent traffic leaks from the funnel.

## Mistake 8: No segmentation by device/time/geo

Lead quality often differs dramatically by device or time window. Bid adjustments should reflect this, especially in local service markets.

## Mistake 9: Reporting only on clicks

Clicks and CPC are not business outcomes. Focus on:

- cost per qualified lead;
- close-rate by campaign cluster;
- share of low-intent inquiries;
- return by intent segment.

## Mistake 10: Ads disconnected from SEO and content

Ads are strongest when paired with SEO architecture. Paid campaigns validate intent quickly, SEO compounds demand over time, and both channels improve landing pages together.

## 30-day recovery plan

1. Rebuild campaign architecture by intent.
2. Validate tracking end-to-end.
3. Refresh negatives and ad assets.
4. Align ad groups with dedicated landing pages.
5. Run weekly optimizations around lead quality.

If your account spends but pipeline quality stays weak, start by auditing the path from query to lead handoff. That is where hidden performance losses usually appear.
