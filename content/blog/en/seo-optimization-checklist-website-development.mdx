## Why pre-launch SEO is non-negotiable

Fixing architecture after release is expensive. URL changes, canonical conflicts, and template rewrites can delay growth for months. A launch-ready SEO baseline gives search engines clean signals from day one.

## 1) Crawlability and indexability

Validate:

- correct `robots.txt` directives;
- complete `sitemap.xml`;
- no accidental `noindex` on money pages;
- canonical consistency;
- hreflang integrity for multilingual routes.

## 2) Metadata quality

Every key page should have unique:

- title aligned with commercial intent;
- description aligned with click intent;
- OpenGraph and Twitter fields for better sharing snippets.

Avoid repetitive templates or duplicated brand patterns.

## 3) Structured data baseline

For a commercial digital studio website:

- `Organization` and `LocalBusiness`;
- `WebSite`;
- `Service` on service pages;
- `FAQPage` where FAQ sections exist;
- `BreadcrumbList` for hierarchical routes.

Content routes should include `BlogPosting` or `Article`.

## 4) Semantic structure

Check technical HTML:

- one `<main>` per page;
- one clear `<h1>`;
- meaningful `h2/h3` hierarchy;
- no hidden heading duplicates.

Semantic consistency improves both accessibility and crawler understanding.

## 5) Core Web Vitals readiness

Before launch, measure:

- LCP on primary landing pages;
- CLS from hero/media/font behavior;
- INP on interactive sections;
- route-level client bundle size.

Performance debt is an SEO debt for commercial pages.

## 6) Local SEO readiness

If local demand matters:

- include city and region context in key pages;
- maintain consistent NAP data;
- define `areaServed` and geocoordinates in schema;
- build content around local commercial demand.

## 7) Conversion readiness

Traffic growth without conversion architecture does not produce ROI. Validate:

- clear CTA in hero and decision sections;
- trust and social proof blocks;
- internal links from informational content to service pages;
- analytics events for every lead-critical action.

## Final launch checklist

1. Full crawl test.
2. Metadata and canonical review.
3. Structured data validation.
4. Lighthouse checks on key URLs.
5. Lead tracking end-to-end test.

If these steps are complete, your launch is SEO-ready and conversion-ready, not just visually complete.
